# Growing beets

Beets are one of the easiest roots to grow, and both halves of the plant are food.

## Sowing

Sow straight into the bed once the soil has warmed to about 10°C, about 2 cm deep, in loose soil free of stones. What looks like one seed is a cluster of several, so each one comes up as a little clump.

## Thinning

When the seedlings are a few centimetres tall, thin each clump to one plant, about 10 cm apart. Do not throw the thinnings away: they are the first salad leaves of the season.

## Watering

Keep the soil evenly moist. A beet that dries out and then floods grows woody rings and splits.

## Harvesting

Pull them from about 50 days, anywhere between golf ball and tennis ball size. Small beets are the sweetest; giant ones are a trophy and not much else.
