# Storing beets

- **Separate the greens straight away.** Twist them off, leaving a few centimetres of stem so the root does not bleed. The leaves pull moisture out of the root if left on.
- **Roots:** unwashed, in a bag in the fridge's crisper, for two to three weeks. In a cool cellar, packed in damp sand, they last for months.
- **Greens:** wrapped in a damp towel in the fridge, and eaten within two or three days, like chard.
- **Cooked beets:** in a closed container in the fridge for up to five days.
