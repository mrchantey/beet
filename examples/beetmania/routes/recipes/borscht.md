# Borscht

There are as many borschts as there are grandmothers. This one is plain, quick and vegetarian.

| ingredient | amount |
| --- | --- |
| beets, peeled and grated | 3 |
| potatoes, diced | 2 |
| carrot, grated | 1 |
| onion, diced | 1 |
| cabbage, shredded | a quarter |
| vegetable stock | 1.5 litres |
| red wine vinegar | 1 tablespoon |
| sour cream and dill | to serve |

1. Soften the onion and carrot in a little oil in a large pot.
2. Add the beets, stir for a few minutes, then add the stock and potatoes and simmer for 15 minutes.
3. Add the cabbage and simmer another 10 minutes, until everything is tender.
4. Stir in the vinegar, which keeps the colour bright, and season to taste.

Serve with sour cream and dill. It tastes better the next day, so make the full pot.
