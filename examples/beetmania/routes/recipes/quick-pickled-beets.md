# Quick pickled beets

A fridge pickle, not a canning project: no sterilising, no waiting weeks.

| ingredient | amount |
| --- | --- |
| [roasted beets](/recipes/roasted-beets), sliced | 4 |
| apple cider vinegar | 1 cup |
| water | 1/2 cup |
| sugar | 2 tablespoons |
| salt | 1 teaspoon |

1. Pack the sliced beets into a clean jar.
2. Warm the vinegar, water, sugar and salt until the sugar dissolves, then pour it over the beets.
3. Cool, close the jar and refrigerate. They are good after an hour, better the next day, and keep for two weeks.

A few slices of red onion or a bay leaf in the jar never hurt.
