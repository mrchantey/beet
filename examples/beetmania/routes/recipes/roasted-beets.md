# Roasted beets

Roasting concentrates a beet's sweetness and loosens its skin, so this is the recipe the others lean on.

| ingredient | amount |
| --- | --- |
| beets, about the size of a fist | 4 |
| olive oil | 1 tablespoon |
| salt | a pinch |

1. Heat the oven to 200°C.
2. Scrub the beets, trim the leaves to a short stub and leave the root tail on, so they do not bleed.
3. Rub them with the oil and salt, wrap them together in foil and roast for 45 to 60 minutes, until a knife slides in.
4. Let them cool until you can hold them, then rub the skins off with a paper towel.

Slice them warm with butter, or keep them in the fridge for up to five days for salads and the [borscht](/recipes/borscht).
