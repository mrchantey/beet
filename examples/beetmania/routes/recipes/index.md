# Recipes

Straight from the patch to the plate. Every recipe here starts with whole beets, and none of them needs more than an oven, a pot or a blender.

1. [Roasted beets](/recipes/roasted-beets): the one to learn first, and the start of most of the others.
2. [Quick pickled beets](/recipes/quick-pickled-beets): sharp, sweet and ready by dinner.
3. [Borscht](/recipes/borscht): a pot of soup that turns everything it touches crimson.

Wear an apron. Beet juice comes out of most things with cold water and patience, and out of wooden boards not at all.
