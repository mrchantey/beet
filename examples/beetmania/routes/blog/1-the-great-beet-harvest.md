The autumn harvest is in. Three rows, three varieties, and one wheelbarrow that will never be the same colour again.

## The patch this year

We sowed in late spring, thinned twice and watered more than we meant to. The **Detroit Dark Reds** did what they always do, the *Chioggias* came up candy-striped, and the golden beets sulked until the last fortnight, then caught up. The [growing guide](/guides/growing) has the details.

The thinnings went into salads all summer, which is the best argument for sowing too thickly.

### What went wrong

A dry week in January split a handful of roots. They still roasted fine.

## The tally

## What happens to them

The roots, in order:

1. roast a tray for the week
2. pickle what the tray cannot hold
3. give the rest to the neighbours, who have stopped answering the door

What we would do differently:

- sow the golden beets a fortnight earlier
- mulch before the dry spell, not after
- buy a second wheelbarrow

## A word from the patch

> A beet is a vegetable that has made its peace with being purple.
