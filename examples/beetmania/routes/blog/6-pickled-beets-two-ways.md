Both jars start the same way: a bunch of beets simmered whole until a knife slides in, then slipped from their skins and sliced.

## The slow jar

Equal parts cider vinegar and water, a spoon of sugar, a cinnamon stick and four cloves, brought to a boil and poured over the slices. It wants a week in the fridge, and it is worth the week.

## The quick jar

White vinegar, plenty of salt and a thumb of fresh horseradish, grated straight into the jar. It is ready by dinner, and it clears the sinuses.

The spare brine from either one is the start of a good [borscht](/recipes/borscht).
